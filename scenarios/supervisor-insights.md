# Scenario: Supervisor Insights & Coaching

## Overview

Supervisors face an impossible task: manually monitoring dozens of concurrent interactions, identifying quality issues in real time, coaching agents based on sparse sampled data, and managing SLA compliance — all at once.

Contact Center Foundry provides supervisors with **AI-powered intelligence** across the entire team interaction stream, transforming reactive supervision into proactive leadership.

---

## Supervisor Pain Points Addressed

| Pain Point | Traditional State | With Contact Center Foundry |
|---|---|---|
| Can only monitor 1–2 interactions at once | Miss most quality issues | AI monitors all interactions simultaneously |
| Coaching based on small samples | Feedback is anecdotal | Evidence-based coaching on full data set |
| Escalations arrive without context | Must re-read full thread | AI delivers instant context summary |
| No real-time risk visibility | Learn about issues after the fact | Live sentiment and risk dashboard |
| Performance reporting is manual | Takes hours to compile | AI-generated reports in seconds |

---

## Feature 2: Interaction Monitoring with AI Context

When a supervisor clicks on any interaction, they see:

- **AI-generated interaction summary** (what has happened so far, current issue, customer sentiment history)
- **Compliance alerts** with specific flagged moments
- **AI suggestion history** (what was suggested to the agent and whether it was used)
- **Customer context** (CRM data, case history, tier)

This context loads in under 2 seconds — no need to read transcripts manually.

## Feature 4: Post-Interaction Quality Review

After each interaction, the supervisor receives:

- AI-generated quality score against the configured rubric
- Specific moments flagged for praise or coaching
- Side-by-side view of what the AI suggested and what the agent said

This enables **100% interaction review** at scale, replacing the traditional 5–10% sample review.

---

## Feature 5: Coaching Intelligence

Over time, the platform builds agent-level performance profiles:

| Insight Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Consistent gap | "M. Williams misses the mandatory security disclosure in 35% of cases — specifically on chat" |
| Knowledge pattern | "3 agents in Team A consistently struggle with billing dispute queries — knowledge gap or training?" |
| Improvement trend | "J. Smith's FCR improved +12% since last coaching session on active listening" |
| Benchmark | "Team B's AHT on technical queries is 20% lower than Team A — investigate best practice" |

Coaching recommendations are generated weekly and surfaced in the supervisor's planning dashboard.
