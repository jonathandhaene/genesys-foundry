# Value & ROI Model

## Executive Summary

Genesys Surround delivers measurable return on investment through efficiency improvements, quality uplift, and compliance risk reduction — typically achieving **payback within 12–18 months** of full deployment.

---

## Value Dimensions

### 1. Agent Productivity

| Metric | Baseline | With AI | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average Handle Time (AHT) | 6 min | 4.5–5 min | -15% to -25% |
| After-Call Work (ACW) | 4 min | 1–2 min | -50% to -70% |
| First Contact Resolution (FCR) | 72% | 80–85% | +8–13% |
| New agent time-to-competency | 8 weeks | 5–6 weeks | -25% to -35% |

**Financial Impact (illustrative: 200-agent contact center)**:
- AHT reduction (20%): ~8,000 agent-hours saved per year → £320k–£480k at £40–60/hour fully loaded
- ACW reduction (60%): ~4,800 agent-hours saved per year → £190k–£290k
- FCR improvement (10%): ~15,000 fewer repeat contacts per year → £300k–£450k at average cost per contact

**Indicative annual productivity value**: £800k–£1.2M for 200 agents

---

### 2. Quality & Compliance

| Metric | Baseline | With AI | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| QA coverage (% interactions reviewed) | 5–8% | 100% | +92–95 pts |
| Compliance breach detection rate | ~20% | ~75% | +55 pts |
| Time to identify compliance breach | Days–weeks | Real-time | Near-instantaneous |
| Cost per QA review | £15–25 | £0.50–1.50 | -90% to -95% |

**Financial Impact**:
- QA cost reduction: £15k–£25k per 1,000 reviewed interactions per year
- Compliance breach reduction: Avoided regulatory fines, remediation cost, and reputational risk (highly variable; significant for regulated industries)
- Reduced E&O claims: Improved accuracy and disclosure compliance reduces claims risk

---

### 3. Knowledge & Self-Service

| Metric | Baseline | With AI | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent knowledge search time | 2–4 min per interaction | 15–30 sec | -80% to -90% |
| Knowledge article currency | Audited annually | Gap-detected continuously | Continuous |
| Escalation rate (due to knowledge gap) | 18% | 10–12% | -6–8 pts |

---

### 4. Strategic / Platform Value

| Benefit | Description | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **System independence** | AI platform survives CRM migration | Avoids £500k–2M AI rebuild cost at next CRM change |
| **Unified AI investment** | One platform vs. per-product AI | Avoids duplicated AI spend across 3–5 systems |
| **Talent retention** | Agents find AI-assisted work less frustrating | Reduced attrition; hiring/training cost avoidance |
| **Scalability** | Handle more volume without proportional headcount increase | Enables digital growth without cost scaling |

## Investment Model

### Phase 1 (Foundation – 3 months)
- Platform deployment (Azure infrastructure, core services)
- Genesys + one CRM integration (e.g., D365 or Salesforce)
- Agent copilot and knowledge service live
- **Typical investment**: £150k–£300k (professional services + Azure)

### Phase 2 (Expansion – 3–6 months)
- All CRM integrations
- Supervisor intelligence dashboard
- Quality scoring automation
- **Additional investment**: £100k–£200k

### Phase 3 (Intelligence – 6–12 months)
- Predictive analytics
- Knowledge curation automation
- Advanced compliance monitoring
- **Additional investment**: £75k–£150k

### Ongoing Azure Run Costs
- Estimated monthly Azure consumption: £5k–£20k (highly dependent on interaction volume)
- Scales linearly with interaction volume; no large fixed infrastructure cost

*Note: These are indicative ranges based on industry benchmarks. Actual ROI will vary based on contact center scale, current performance baselines, industry, and deployment scope. A detailed business case workshop is recommended before investment commitment.*

## Risk-Adjusted Value

Unlike point solutions, Genesys Surround provides **platform-level value** that:

1. **Compounds over time** — each new CRM or channel added increases value without rebuilding AI
2. **Reduces stranded investment risk** — platform survives system changes
3. **Creates organisational capability** — AI becomes a repeatable enterprise competency, not a one-time project
