# Digital Transformation Narrative

## The Contact Center AI Dilemma

Most enterprises are caught between two equally unsatisfying approaches to AI in the contact center:

**Option A: Buy AI from the contact center vendor**  
→ You get AI that only works in that one platform. When you change platforms (or add another), the AI investment is lost.

**Option B: Buy AI from each CRM/ITSM vendor**  
→ You get fragmented AI. Three different knowledge bases. Three different governance frameworks. Three times the cost. No unified view of the customer.

Both options share the same fatal flaw: **AI is treated as a feature of a system, not as an enterprise capability**.

---

## A Different Way of Thinking

Genesys Surround is built on a different premise:

> **AI should sit above your systems — not inside them.**

Just as you have a shared data platform, a shared identity platform, and a shared network — you should have a **shared AI platform for customer operations**.

This platform:
- Connects to all your systems (but is owned by none of them)
- Serves all your personas (agents, supervisors, knowledge managers, compliance teams)
- Governs AI consistently (one framework, one audit trail, one model governance process)
- Survives system changes (new CRM? new contact center? The AI platform stays)

## Why Now?

Several converging factors make this the right moment for platform AI in contact centers:

1. **LLM maturity**: GPT-4o class models are reliable, fast, and controllable enough for enterprise deployment
2. **Azure OpenAI data governance**: Enterprise data isolation and no-training guarantees remove the main CISO blocker
3. **CRM proliferation**: Most enterprises now use 2–4 CRM/ITSM systems — the fragmentation problem has peaked
4. **Regulatory pressure**: GDPR, EU AI Act, and sector regulations make governance-first AI not optional but mandatory
5. **Workforce pressure**: Agent attrition and recruitment difficulty make AI assistance a talent retention tool
