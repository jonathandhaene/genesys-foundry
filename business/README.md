## Summary Business Case

Genesys Surround delivers measurable value across three dimensions:

### Efficiency
- Agents resolve issues faster with AI assistance (AHT reduction: 15–30%)
- After-call work reduced by up to 70% through AI-generated case notes
- Quality review coverage increases from ~5% to 100% without adding headcount

### Customer Experience
- Consistent, accurate answers regardless of which agent answers
- Faster resolution reduces customer effort and increases satisfaction
- Proactive escalation and churn signals addressed before customer leaves

### Compliance & Risk
- 100% interaction monitoring vs. 5–10% sample
- Real-time compliance flagging reduces breach risk
- Comprehensive audit trail simplifies regulatory reporting

## Strategic Value
- AI investment is **shared across all systems** — not duplicated per vendor
- Platform **survives CRM or contact center changes** — AI capabilities persist
- Enables **data-driven transformation** of contact center operations
